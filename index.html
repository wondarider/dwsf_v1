DIGITAL WELLBEING

SECURITY FRAMEWORK

Protecting children in an Unfiltered Digital World

In today's digital landscape, over 70% of youth have experienced some form of online harm, prompting governments worldwide to implement measures such as social media bans to protect their communities.

With clear risk visibility, prioritized resource allocation, and proven duty of care for an entire school community we can equip children, teachers, and parents with the tools and knowledge needed to safely navigate the digital world.

Our project stands at the forefront of the global movement of banning social media for youths - transitioning from reactive crisis management to proactive digital resilience.

Together, we can create a safer online environment and foster responsible digital citizenship.

We shall be trialing the assessment with schools soon - if you would like to know more or to get involved please reach out!

Digital Safety & Maturity Model

Continuous Alignment Across Key Community Stakeholders

Pillar 01

1. Educated Child

Empowers students with digital literacy, online risk recognition, self-regulation, and positive citizenship behavior.

Target Score 3.0 / 4.0
Pillar 02

2. Empowered Teachers

Equips educators with training, classroom guidance tools, and curriculum resources to mentor students safely.

Target Score 3.0 / 4.0
Pillar 03

3. Engaged Parents

Aligns home monitoring, clear family rules, and open communication with the school's digital policies.

Target Score 3.0 / 4.0
Pillar 04

4. Accessible Help

Provides clear, non-judgmental support paths, rapid reporting channels, and escalation procedures when issues arise.

Target Score 3.0 / 4.0
Overarching Foundation

5. Governance

Connects and sustains all four pillars through official safety policies, data privacy compliance, leadership accountability, continuous stakeholder evaluation, and resource allocation.

Framework Scope Policy, Risk Audit & Continuous Compliance

How the Assessment Works

1

Self-Diagnostic

Complete a targeted assessment evaluating digital wellbeing governance and safety training programs.

➔
2

Maturity Scoring

Responses are measured across 4 maturity tiers: Ad-hoc, Reactive, Defined, and Optimized.

➔
3

Gap Analysis

Identify critical blind spots across student safety, parent engagement, technical controls, and governance.

➔
4

Action Roadmap

Receive a practical, prioritised action plan tailored to raise your school's online safety maturity level.

Benefits of a Digital Wellbeing Assessment

Clear Baseline

Understand your actual protection capabilities instead of relying on assumptions or misaligned policies.

Prioritised Budgeting

Direct limited resources and prioritize investment exactly where risks are highest and operational gaps are evident.

Stakeholder Confidence

Demonstrate proactive duty of care to parents, school boards, and education regulators.

Proactive Culture

Shift school operations from reacting to online crises toward sustained prevention and individual resilience.
