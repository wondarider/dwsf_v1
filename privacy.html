Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Overview

The Digital Wellbeing Security Framework (DWSF) is a self-funded benchmarking tool that helps schools assess and improve their digital safety maturity. This policy explains how DWSF handles information in connection with this website and the assessment framework.

What information DWSF does not collect or store

DWSF does not collect, store, process, or have access to:

  • Student personal information or records
  • Staff or parent personal information
  • Assessment responses, scores, or results
  • Any sensitive or identifiable data relating to a school community

All assessment activity — including responses, scoring, and any resulting reports — takes place entirely within the school's own infrastructure, systems, or nominated tools. DWSF provides the framework, methodology, and guidance materials only. Schools remain fully responsible for how they administer the assessment, and for the storage, security, and handling of any data it generates.

Information collected via this website

This website may collect limited, non-sensitive information in the following circumstances:

  • Contact form / email enquiries: If you email us (e.g. via the "Get Involved" button), we receive whatever information you choose to include — typically your name, email address, and message content. This is used only to respond to your enquiry and is not shared with third parties.

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes, and do not sell or share any information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data security

Because assessment data never passes through or is stored on DWSF-operated systems, the security and integrity of that data is governed by each school's own IT policies and infrastructure — not by DWSF.

For the limited contact information DWSF does receive (e.g. enquiry emails), reasonable steps are taken to protect it, though as a self-funded personal project, no enterprise-grade security certification is claimed.

Third-party links

This website links to external resources (e.g. eSafety Commissioner, Cyberbullying Research Center). DWSF is not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites.

Children's privacy

DWSF's website and framework are intended for use by school staff and administrators, not directly by children. DWSF does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact: contact@digitalwellbeingsecurityframework.com
